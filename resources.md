The current idea we have is a platform that allow users to submit pictures of the problems they find in the streets, public spaces... we want to make it easy for users to contribute in this platform , thats why we choose a mobile first approach, the user can take a picture and submit it to the platform, and the platform will use AI to analyze the picture and identify the problem, then it will assign the problem to the relevant authority to fix it. the ai should give a title, description, category and alll useful informations from the picture, the user have three ways to submit picture coordinates on the interactive map, first GPS access, second manually pin pointing on the map and third by extracting the coordiantes from the picture metadata, the platform is a an interactive map just like fixmystreet or poop map
